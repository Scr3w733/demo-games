## Short description (EN)

A dark HD-2D pixel survivor. Light is your only ally: keep the torch burning, fend off waves of the undead and descend deeper into the necropolis.

## Short description (RU)

Мрачный HD-2D пиксельный survivor. Свет — твой единственный союзник: держи факел горящим, отбивай волны нежити и спускайся глубже в некрополь.

## Long description (EN)

NECROPOLIS: Last Light is a browser survivor game built entirely with procedural pixel art and WebAudio — no paid assets, no external music files.

- **10 classes**, each with three unique abilities. Rare classes unlock from cases.
- **1028 weapons** with rarity and wear, 3 module slots, skins and equipment.
- **1000 named campaign levels** — survive 60 seconds to clear each one.
- **Game modes:** classic survival, blitz, nightmare, boss rush, "Silence".
- **City cleanup** (opened at profile level 5): 54 multi-storey buildings to enter and clear.
- **Open world** (level 5): 14400x10200 map with forests, swamps, ruins and snow.
- **Base:** shelter with rooms, portals, NPCs, forge and upgrades that carry into every run.
- **HD-2D visuals:** soft darkness mask, radial light sources, additive glows, flashlight cone with battery, candles, lanterns and braziers.
- **Full localization:** Russian and English; rebindable keys, touch joystick on mobile, adjustable graphics quality.

Everything is generated in code: sprites, effects and the soundtrack. Progress is saved locally in the browser.

## Controls (for the listing)

WASD / arrows — move; mouse or J — shoot; Space — dash; Q / E — abilities; R — ultimate; F — medkit; T — flashlight; G — interact; Esc — pause; M — minimap. All keys are rebindable in-game; touch joystick is available on mobile.

## Notes for the platforms

- Upload `grimhollow-html5.zip` as an HTML5 game; `index.html` sits at the archive root and all paths are relative (`base: './'`).
- Entrypoint: `index.html`. Do **not** upload `landing.html` — that's the promotional site.
- Games runs offline in the browser and needs no server or backend.
- The Yandex Games SDK wrapper falls back to a demo mode outside the Yandex iframe, so the build works on other portals.
- No real payments and no email sending are implemented; don't claim them in the listing.
- Fonts are SIL OFL (licence files are in `fonts/`), tiles are CC0 (`assets/LICENSE-CC0.md`).
